## Suggested submission description

Bots is a private, self-hosted monitoring dashboard for Unraid. Monitor product prices and stock, HTTP websites, and TCP servers. Bots includes confirmation rules, response-time and price history, outage and recovery alerts, DNS and TLS monitoring, and Discord, Gotify, Pushover, Telegram, SMTP, and webhook notifications. All durable data stays in the mapped `/data` folder.
